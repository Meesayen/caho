# Cards Against Humanity – Online

I worked on this side project during the 2020 pandemic. I wanted a way to
play Cards Against Humanity with my friends remotely, and I also took the
chance to have some fun with React, after years of background in other
frameworks (Vue, Angular).

I never fully finished or polished it, but the game loop was working and
fun. We managed to play a few games, defying the pandemic that kept us
apart.
